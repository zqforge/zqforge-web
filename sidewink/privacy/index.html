Sidewink · Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated September 30, 2026. This notice describes the current Sidewink iPhone build and must be reviewed again if the app changes before release.

Playing on one phone

Sidewink's current Quick Party games use player names, choices, votes, and scores on your iPhone during the game. The app does not require an account. It does not send this game information to a Sidewink server or save a game history. Ending the game or closing the app clears the current in-memory session.

Support email

If you choose to email us, your email address, message, and any attachments are delivered through your email provider to Sidewink. We use them to respond to your request. Please avoid including other people's personal information, private game details, or room invitations. You can request access to or deletion of a support message by writing to sidewink@zqforge.com. We will review the request and explain if any information must be retained.

Services and permissions

The current Release build has no advertising or analytics SDK and does not use the camera or local network for its playable games. It does not offer a purchase in the app. Device diagnostics and your chosen email service are handled by Apple and your email provider under their own terms.

Changes and contact

If Sidewink adds multiplayer gameplay, purchases, persistent history, analytics, or other services, this notice will be updated to describe the version you use. For privacy questions, write to sidewink@zqforge.com.

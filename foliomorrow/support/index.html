FolioMorrow for iPhone

Support

Practical help for importing, reviewing, connecting, and protecting your document history.

Contact

Email foliomorrow@zqforge.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, FolioMorrow version, what you expected, and the steps that reproduced the issue. Do not attach a private document unless necessary and authorized.

Import a document

Use Add to choose a PDF or image from Files or Photos, scan with the camera, or send a supported item through the iOS share sheet. Keep FolioMorrow open while the import finishes. If extraction needs review, open the document and confirm or correct the highlighted fields.

Connect a newer document

FolioMorrow may suggest that a document belongs to the same policy, lease, vehicle, identity, warranty, contract, or subscription. Review the evidence before confirming. You can remove a relationship or reconsider an ignored suggestion later.

Ask about a document

Open a document or life area and use its focused Ask feature. Answers are intentionally bounded to imported evidence. If FolioMorrow cannot support an answer from the available documents, it will say so instead of guessing.

Reminders

FolioMorrow uses local notifications only after you allow them. Review due dates before enabling an action. You can change notification permission in iOS Settings and manage or delete actions inside FolioMorrow.

Export and sharing

FolioMorrow exports an original document only when you choose Share or Export. Check the destination before sending. The receiving app or service handles the copy under its own privacy terms.

Purchases and Restore Purchases

FolioMorrow Pro is available as an annual auto-renewable subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. There is no free trial. Apple manages billing, renewal, cancellation, and payment details.

If a previous purchase is not recognized, open FolioMorrow's purchase screen and choose Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Cancelling a subscription does not delete documents or results already stored on your device.

Delete local data

Deleting a document removes its FolioMorrow copy and derived records, but it does not delete the original file or photo outside the app. FolioMorrow has no account or cloud sync, so deleted local data cannot be restored by ZQ Forge.

Privacy

Read the FolioMorrow Privacy Policy for details about on-device processing, permissions, storage, sharing, and deletion.
